Supply Chain Intelligence Platform

A five-page Power BI model covering the full chain — procurement spend, inventory health, logistics performance and supplier risk — built so an operations lead can go from a headline number to the specific product, supplier or route behind it.

Power BI SQL · Python 50 suppliers · 6 sites · 16.5K shipments

Executive overview

Revenue, inventory value and shipment health on one screen — the view a supply-chain lead opens first.

Procurement and supplier spend

Where the money goes and who it goes to: spend by category and supplier, average lead time, and a quality score per category.

Inventory health

Dead stock and every product sitting below its safety stock level, by warehouse — the working list for a replenishment run.

Logistics and transit

Shipment status, transit time by mode, transportation cost by route, delay reasons, and a geographic view of shipment origins.

Risk and recommendations

Supplier risk scores, a delay-prediction view by route, inventory trajectory, and per-supplier action prompts.

How it was built

Model

A star schema over shipments, purchase orders, inventory positions and supplier master data, related on product, supplier, warehouse and date keys.

Measures

DAX measures for spend, lead time, turnover, on-time rate and safety-stock breaches, written once and reused across all five pages.

Navigation

Each page answers one question and drills to the row level — the safety-stock page lists the exact product and warehouse, not just a count.

Data

Simulated operational data generated to mirror a multi-site manufacturer, so the model can be shared without exposing a real supply chain.
